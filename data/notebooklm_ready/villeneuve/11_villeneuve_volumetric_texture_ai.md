## Expert Analysis

### Volumetric Architecture

#### Concept

Atmosphere as physical structure (fog, dust, smog)

#### Lighting Physics

Light must travel 'through' matter, creating visible beams (god rays)

#### Scale Through Obscurity

Using atmospheric haze to purposefully obscure distant details, implying infinite scale

### Textural Brutalism

#### Surface Detail

Concrete weathering, sand grain physics, industrial decay

#### Visual Tactility

High-frequency detail in low-contrast environments


## Ai Video Generation Implications

### Noise Scheduler Tuning

#### Grain Simulation

Injecting specific Gaussian noise distributions at final diffusion steps to mimic Kodak film stocks

#### Prompting Texture

'Volumetric fog', 'suspended dust particles', 'haptic texture', 'concrete porosity'

### Depth Haziness

#### Z Depth Fog

Conditioning generation with depth maps where far-field density > near-field

#### Color Grading

Desaturated palettes (orange/teal) applied at latent generation level

### Generative Upscaling

#### Risk

AI upscalers often 'clean' texture; Villeneuve style requires preserving 'dirty' industrial textures

#### Technique

Texture-aware upscaling trained on brutalist architectural datasets
