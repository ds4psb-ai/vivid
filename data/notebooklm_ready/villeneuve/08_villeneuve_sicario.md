**Auteur:** Denis Villeneuve | **Category:** film_analysis | **ID:** villeneuve_sicario

## Visual Approach

### Cinematographer

Roger Deakins

### Core Contrast

Cruel harshness of sunlight vs menacing unknown of shadows

### Villeneuve Quote

Could shoot with a shoe and it would look great


## Border Tension

### Landscape As Character

Vast imposing Mexican border

### Effect

Overwhelming chaos

### Wide Shots

#### Example

Raid team disappearing over ridge

#### Planning

Storyboarded weeks in advance

#### Description

Disappearing into the blackness


## Violence Portrayal

### Perspective

Point of view of victims

### Approach

Shows brutality without glorifying


## Tunnel Sequence

### Night Vision Approach

#### Equipment

Actual thermal vision cameras + night vision lens enhancers

#### Rationale

Characters would rely on these in darkness

#### Commitment

Edgy reality over faked moonlight

### Thermal Imaging

#### Character

Alejandro (Benicio Del Toro)

#### Distinction

Different from standard green night vision

#### Purpose

Distinguish his viewpoint

### Minimal Lighting

#### Setup

Single 1k lamp at large reflector

#### Result

Just enough shape for image-enhancing devices


## Tension Building

### Technique

Holding shots

### Effect

Let audience feel suspense vs rapid cuts

### Example

Team descent held until they disappear


## Lean Style

### Focus

What is important without extraneous elements

### Single Camera

Deakins' preferred method

### Pre Production

Considerable storyboarding and discussion
