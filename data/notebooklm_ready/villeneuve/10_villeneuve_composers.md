# Denis Villeneuve Composer Collaborations - Johannsson and Zimmer
**Auteur:** Denis Villeneuve | **Category:** sound_and_score | **ID:** villeneuve_composer_collaborations

---

## Villeneuve Music Philosophy

### View

Music as crucial storytelling tool

### Goal

Immerse audience, evoke specific emotions

### Approach

Composers brought in early, given creative freedom

### Blur Technique

Lines between reality and unreality in sound


## Johann Johannsson

### Films

- Prisoners (2013)
- Sicario (2015)
- Arrival (2016)

### Style

#### Blend

Traditional orchestration with electronic elements

#### Characteristics

- Minimalist
- Unusual chords
- Tense
- Somber
- Profound

### Prisoners

#### Elements

- Low drones
- Distinctive cello melody
- Choral/hymnal approach

#### Themes

Grief and despair

### Sicario

#### Request

Subtle war music

#### Result

Brooding, brutal, intense, relentless

#### Technique

Layering acoustical strings with electronica

### Arrival

#### Elements

- Orchestral
- Experimental
- Human voices
- Otherworldly tones

#### Themes

Communication and time

### Texture Obsession

Striving for minimal forms, emotional resonance


## Blade Runner 2049 Transition

### Original Composer

Johannsson

### Outcome

Parted ways

### Reason

Film needed something different, closer to Vangelis


## Hans Zimmer

### Films

- Blade Runner 2049 (with Wallfisch)
- Dune (2021)
- Dune Part Two (2024)

### Approach

Method composer - immerse in film's world

### Technique

Blur scoring and sound design


## Dune Score

### Goal

Reflect alien planet and future

### Rejection

Traditional European orchestra sounds

### Innovations

#### Instruments

Invented instruments and sounds that didn't exist

#### Voices

Manipulated female voices as base

#### Language

Developed unique musical language

### Effect

Emotion through sometimes simple harmonies
