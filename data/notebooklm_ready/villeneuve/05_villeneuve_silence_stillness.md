## Unsettling Atmosphere

### Core Approach

Something ominous looms without explicit events

### Ambiguity

Designed to keep viewers in uncertainty

### Psychological Effect

Pervasive sense of dread and unease


## Silence And Stillness

### Technique

#### Rhythm

#### Atmosphere

## Negative Space

### Purpose

Foster vulnerability and anxiety during crisis

### Enemy

#### Framing

Abundance of empty space above or behind subject

#### Effect

Diminishes physical presence, injects mystery

### Sicario

#### Kate Framing

Significant negative space around protagonist

#### Effect

Emphasizes isolation and diminished agency

### Off Center Composition

#### Technique

Characters placed off-center creating unbalanced frames

#### Subconscious Effect

Feeling of unease
