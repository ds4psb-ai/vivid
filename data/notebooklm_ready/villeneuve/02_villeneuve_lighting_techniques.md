# Denis Villeneuve Lighting Techniques - Roger Deakins Collaboration
**Auteur:** Denis Villeneuve | **Category:** cinematography | **ID:** villeneuve_lighting_techniques

---

## Blade Runner 2049

### Collaboration

#### Cinematographer

Roger Deakins (Academy Award winner)

#### Integration

Lighting designed with set architecture from Day One

#### Vfx Involvement

Deakins supervised VFX for visual integrity

### Lighting Philosophy

#### Naturalistic Stylized

Natural look in highly stylized futuristic world

#### In Camera

Achieve color effects in-camera, not post

#### Practical Emphasis

Custom fixtures designed for each set

### Signature Techniques

#### Moving Light

##### Purpose

Create sense of artificiality and dynamism

##### Context

Dystopian techno-future with scarce natural sunlight

##### Effect

Constant movement = artificial world

#### Water Caustics

##### Location

Wallace's office (platform surrounded by water)

##### Equipment

10K Fresnel lamps high above water

##### Effect

Moving light patterns on walls

##### Enhancement

Steam from heated water for atmosphere

#### Ring Lights

##### Location

Niander Wallace office

##### Construction

256 ARRI 300-watt Fresnels in concentric circles

##### Programming

Slow chase effect mimicking skylight sunlight

##### Effect

Soft broad light with focused intensity

### Camera Techniques

#### Wide Lenses

##### Usage

14mm lenses to exaggerate scale

##### Purpose

Convey vastness of futuristic environments

##### Sets

Very large, built on stages

#### Silhouette Design

##### Example

Opening scene: Gosling kept in silhouette

##### Method

Lighting + architecture combined

#### Fog And Mist

##### Prevalence

Heavy use throughout interior/exterior

##### Effect

Oppressive, mysterious atmosphere


## Dune Influence

### Cinematographer

Greig Fraser

### Villeneuve Quote

Working with Deakins was formative experience

### Lasting Impact

Instinctively thinking about light during VFX

### Fraser Approach

Optimize emotional quality of light, character-based lens choices


## Deakins Principles

### Practical Sources

Motivate light from existing or custom sources

### Minimal Post

Heavy emphasis on in-camera capture

### Detail Preservation

Practical shooting maintains weight and detail
