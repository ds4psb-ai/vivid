**Auteur:** Quentin Tarantino | **Category:** cinematography | **ID:** tarantino_cinematography_techniques

## Collaboration

### Cinematographer

Robert Richardson (since Kill Bill)

### Films

- Kill Bill Vol. 1 & 2
- Inglourious Basterds
- Django Unchained
- The Hateful Eight
- Once Upon a Time in Hollywood

### Dynamic

Tarantino specific about shots, Richardson executes


## Richardson Style

### Bold Lighting

#### Signature

Strong rim lighting with top lights

#### Effect

Halo effect around subjects

#### Technique

Overexpose rim light (especially effective on film)

#### Unmotivated

Not always seeking motivated lighting

### Operator Philosophy

#### Approach

Personally operates camera (rides Pegasus crane)

#### Reason

Intimate connection to visual storytelling

#### Actor Eye Contact

Crucial for understanding performance


## Signature Shots

### Trunk Shot

#### Description

Low-angle POV from car trunk

#### Effect

Characters appear intimidating

#### Focus

Dialogue and performance by limiting background

#### Essence

Low-angle POV, not strictly inside trunk

#### Films

- Reservoir Dogs
- Pulp Fiction
- Kill Bill
- Jackie Brown

### Long Takes

#### Planning

Decided early in pre-production

#### Camera Movement

Complex movements (high-angle following actor)

#### Tarantino Role

Writes visuals into script


## Working Process

### No Video Village

#### Tarantino Method

Observes actors directly beside camera

#### Focus

Actor performance over playback

#### Trust

Implicit trust in Richardson as operator

### Script Integration

#### Visuals Written

Camera and composition in script

#### Psychological Impact

Specific about camera height effect


## Lens Choices

### Once Upon Time

#### Camera

Panaflex Millennium XL2

#### Lenses

- Panavision Ts
- Gs
- Cs
- Es
- Primos

#### Aesthetic

1970s retro, slightly off balance with modern twist

#### Format

35mm (for zoom shots, not possible on 70mm)

### Hateful Eight

#### Format

Ultra Panavision 70 (65mm film)

#### Aspect Ratio

2.76:1 (widest available)

#### Purpose

Landscapes out of faces, multiple characters in frame

### Preference

Panavision cameras and lenses
