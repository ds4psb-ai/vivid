### Location

Buenos Aires, Argentina

### Significance

Geographical and metaphorical antipode of Hong Kong

### Symbolism

Opposite of home, displacement

Late-night neon vibe similar to Hong Kong with melancholy aura

Tumultuous romance, attempt to rekindle

Christopher Doyle

### Style

#### Color

Vivid, oversaturated, impressionistic

#### Contrast

Juxtaposed with high-contrast black-and-white
