## Expert Analysis

### Spatial Verticality

#### Concept

Architecture as rigid social stratification

#### Technique

Extreme high/low angle compositions relative to ground level

#### Parasite Mechanics

##### Banjiha Depth

Semi-basement creates 'sub-surface' visual linguistics

##### Mansion Elevation

Modernist glass walls as transparency of power

##### Staircase Motif

Physical labor of social mobility

#### Snowpiercer Linearity

##### Shift

Transformation of vertical class structure to horizontal linearity

##### Motion Vector

Left-to-right constant progression vs static verticality

### Lighting Semantics

#### Natural Light Privilege

Direct correlation between lux/lumens and social class

#### Subterranean Lighting

Green/tungsten biased, high-noise floor aesthetics

#### Shadow Architecture

Shadows used not just for mood but for 'hiding' class markers


## Ai Video Generation Implications

### Depth Map Conditioning

#### Strategy

Use high-contrast depth maps to enforce vertical hierarchy in generation

#### Prompting

Condition Z-axis distinct layers: 'subterranean foreground, street-level midground, elevated modernist background'

#### Controlnet Guidance

Architectural cross-section control for multi-level storytelling

### Lighting As Class Token

#### Rich Token

sun-drenched, volumetric god-rays, 5400K daylight balance, noise-free

#### Poor Token

fluorescent flicker, sodium vapor street light bleed, high ISO grain simulation

### Camera Movement Prompts

#### Descending Tracking

Continuous vertical tilt-down or jib-down to reveal lower stratas

#### Lateral Tracking

Unbroken horizontal tracking for linear struggle (Snowpiercer style)
