# Bong Joon-ho Sound Design and Film Score
**Auteur:** Bong Joon-ho | **Category:** sound_design | **ID:** bong_sound_design

---

## Parasite Sound Design

### Sound Designer

Ralph Tae Young Choi

### Philosophy

#### Minimalism

Tension through minimal sound or deliberate silence

#### Footsteps

Subtle noises carry significant weight

### Class Through Sound

#### Kim Basement

##### Environment

Constant buzz of streets, intricate background noise

##### Meaning

Lack of privacy, urban poverty

#### Park Mansion

##### Environment

Relative silence

##### Meaning

Insulated wealth, privilege

### Rain Metaphor

#### Park Perspective

Pleasant background tick

#### Kim Perspective

Heralds despair and destruction (flooding)

### Door Sounds

#### Technique

Various door sounds in Park house

#### Symbolism

Divide between haves and have-nots

### Artificial Ambient

#### Method

Most ambient noise artificially created

#### Techniques

- Speakers during filming
- Extensive post-production editing


## Parasite Score

### Composer

Jung Jae-il

### Theme

Class inequality and tension

### Musical Progression

#### Opening

Sparse, somber ascending piano motif

#### Meaning

Aspiration of upward mobility

### Baroque Elements

#### Piece

The Belt of Faith

#### Usage

Accompanies Kim family schemes

#### Meaning

Pursuit of wealth, elaborate conspiracy

### Descent Transition

#### Elements

Darker, atmospheric, discordant

#### Hints

Horror elements

#### Bong Description

Nightmare of capitalism

### Conciliation Trilogy

#### Function

Parallels Kim family's evolving conspiracy

#### Effect

Tonal texture and audial symbolism

### Classical Usage

#### Piece

Aria from Rodelinda (opera)

#### Scene

Garden party

#### Effect

Creates alienation, references usurpation


## Memories Of Murder Sound

### Characteristics

#### Predominant

Dialogues and natural sound effects

#### Atmosphere

Continuity maintained

#### Silence

Strategic for suspense generation

### Audio Team

Live Tone

### Bong Philosophy

Realistic with impacts

### Unsettling Absence

Silence emphasizes killer still at large


## Memories Of Murder Score

### Composer

Taro Iwashiro

### Collaboration

20+ demo tapes, multiple meetings with Bong

### Rhythm Style

Almost connected, yet disconnected rhythms

### Purpose

Reflects fragmented police investigation, missing information

### Emotional Function

#### Tense Scenes

Stark, haunting melodies

#### Emotional Toll

Softer, melancholic tunes
