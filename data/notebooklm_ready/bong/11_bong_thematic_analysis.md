# Bong Joon-ho Thematic Analysis - Class, Capitalism, and Monsters
**Auteur:** Bong Joon-ho | **Category:** thematic_analysis | **ID:** bong_thematic_analysis

---

## Class Struggle

### Core Conflict

Eternal battle between rich vs poor, haves vs have-nots

### Approach

Often literalized through physical separation

### Snowpiercer

#### Metaphor

Train as microcosm of capitalist class division

#### Wealthy

Luxurious front cars

#### Poor

Squalid tail section

#### Theme

Rich maintain power by keeping poor in place

### Parasite

#### Setting

Domestic infiltration

#### Nuance

Complex coexistence of different classes

#### Bong Quote

Fear that gap won't be resolved in future

#### Staircase Symbolism

Divisions of social class, power struggles

#### Critique

System implicates everyone - poor want to become rich, not dismantle system

### Pattern

Sons from impoverished families become scapegoats (Memories, Mother)


## Capitalism Critique

### Approach

Sharp social commentary on failings of contemporary capitalism

### Avoids

Overt moralizing

### Parasite Insight

Class aspirationalism, political divide from economic disparity

### Upward Mobility Hostility

Desire to climb, contempt for those below

### System Reproduction

Anti-capitalist narratives focusing on individual can reproduce capitalism's logic


## Monster Genre Subversion

### Purpose

Monster as metaphor for societal ills

### The Host

#### Literal Monster

Creature mutated by toxic waste (Han River formaldehyde)

#### Real Monster

Hidden in mutated minds of those in power

#### Critique

Government incompetence, arbitrary actions of powerful entities

#### Family Focus

Resilience vs failure of official systems

### Okja

#### Real Monster

Multi-national corporation, not the giant pig

#### Theme

Corporate capitalism's impact on individuals and environment

### Technique

Personify systemic problems within genre conventions

### Blend

Sci-fi horror + political satire + family drama


## Social Observation

### Balance

Reality and metaphor in narratives

### Korean Context

#### Shame Culture

Prevalent in Korean society

#### Underclass

Alienation of underclass survivors

### Accurate Observer

True
