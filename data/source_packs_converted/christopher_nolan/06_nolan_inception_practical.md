#### Structure

Hotel corridor built inside rotating drum

#### Filming Time

Full 3 weeks

### Camera Technique

#### Placement

Mounted directly onto rotating set

#### Effect

Seamless zero-gravity simulation

#### Lighting

Integrated into set due to continuous rotation
