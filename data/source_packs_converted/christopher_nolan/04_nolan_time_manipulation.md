## Tenet

### Time Inversion

#### Concept

Entropy of objects/individuals reversed

#### Mechanism

Turnstiles - futuristic devices enabling inversion

### Temporal Pincer

#### Definition

Attack event from forward AND inverted directions simultaneously

#### Effect

Mind-bending action sequences

### Causality Challenge

#### Philosophy

Effects precede causes from normal temporal viewpoint

#### Inverted Entities

Move backward through time


## Interstellar

### Scientific Basis

### Time Dilation

#### Cause

#### Effect

Time passes slower for those near gravity source

### Emotional Consequence

#### Aging Rates

Vast differences between characters

#### Themes

- Mortality
- Loss
- Preciousness of time
