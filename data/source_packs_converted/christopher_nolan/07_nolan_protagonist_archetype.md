# Christopher Nolan Protagonist Archetype - Obsession and Identity
**Auteur:** Christopher Nolan | **Category:** thematic_analysis | **ID:** nolan_protagonist_archetype

---

## Archetype Overview

### Characteristics

- Emotionally disturbed
- Obsessive
- Morally ambiguous

### Driving Force

Singular focus fueled by guilt, trauma, or pursuit of ideal

### Pattern

Broken men chasing obsessions and constructing identities


## Dom Cobb

### Film

Inception

### Obsession

Overwhelming guilt over wife Mal's death, desire to return to children

### Origin

#### Exploration

Shared dream exploration with Mal

#### Limbo

Got lost exploring all possibilities

### Inception On Mal

#### Act

Convinced her dream world wasn't real

#### Consequence

Led to her suicide in real world

### Manifestation

#### Mal Projection

Powerful subconscious guilt

#### Sabotage

Haunts and sabotages his missions

### Ending Choice

Emotional truth over objective reality - doesn't verify if awake


## Bruce Wayne Batman

### Films

The Dark Knight Trilogy

### Obsession

Eradicate crime and corruption in Gotham

### Trauma

Witnessing parents' murder

### Symbol Creation

#### Using Fear

Uses childhood fear of bats

#### Belief

People need dramatic examples to shake them from apathy

#### Goal

Incorruptible, everlasting symbol

### Dual Identity

#### Facade

Public Bruce Wayne persona

#### Truth

True driven self as Batman

### Sacrifice

Limits personal relationships, bears weight of city's sins


## Shared Traits

### Trauma Catalyst

Foundational personal trauma drives action

### Identity Construction

Build/hide behind identity linked to obsession

### Internal Conflict

Battle between right/wrong, reality/illusion

### Emotional Guardedness

Suppress or avoid emotional needs

### Existential Exploration

Questions about reality, memory, identity


## Narrative Function

### Not Plot Device

Central to psychological makeup

### Serves As

Engine of narrative and crucible of moral/existential dilemmas
