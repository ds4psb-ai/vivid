## Pioneering Achievement

### Significance

First major narrative feature extensively integrating IMAX

### Impact

Game-changer for Hollywood blockbusters

### Dark Knight Stats

#### Imax Footage

28 minutes (~15-20% of runtime)

#### Standard Footage

35mm with Panavision anamorphic lenses


## Aspect Ratio Philosophy

### Dynamic Transitions

#### 35Mm Ratio

2.39:1 or 2.40:1 (wider, cinematic)

#### Imax Ratio

1.43:1 or 1.78:1 (taller, filling screen)

#### Effect

Visual expansion during key moments

### Storytelling Purpose

#### Selection Criteria

Moments demanding enhanced immersion

#### Function

Heighten importance of scenes
