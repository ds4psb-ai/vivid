## Oppenheimer Techniques

### Imax Intimate

#### Approach

IMAX for intimate character scenes, not just spectacle

#### Philosophy

Human face as landscape to explore

#### Innovation

First-ever IMAX black-and-white analog film stock (with Kodak)

### Trinity Test

#### Method

Practical effects, no CGI

#### Components

- Miniatures
- Pyrotechnics
- Creative lighting

#### Camera Trick

Bomb placed closer to camera for perceived size

#### Filming

Multiple explosions at high speeds

### Subatomic Visualization

#### Method

Cloud tanks with physical objects

#### Objects

- Spinning beads
- Ball bearings
- Aluminum flakes
- Ping-pong balls

### Custom Lenses

#### Collaborator

Panavision + van Hoytema

#### Type

Custom macro snorkel lenses for IMAX

#### Effect

Capture microscopic details with wide-angle perspective


## Dunkirk Techniques

### Imax Percentage

70-75% shot in IMAX 65mm

### Dialogue Solution

#### Problem

IMAX cameras too loud for dialogue

#### Solution

65mm cameras for quiet scenes

### Camera Innovation

#### Spitfire Cockpit

Custom rigs, periscope lenses

#### Airplane Wing

IMAX mounted on wing for aerial shots

### Extreme Practical

#### Hero Shot

IMAX camera inside stunt plane crashed into ocean

#### Result

Film retrieved after 90 minutes underwater, successfully developed


## Dark Knight Techniques

### Pioneer

First major use of IMAX in blockbuster

### Footage

50+ minutes of IMAX in Dark Knight Rises

### Aspect Ratio

Anamorphic + IMAX combined, central framing for smooth transitions
