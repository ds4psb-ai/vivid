## Dialogue Tension

### Philosophy

Conversations more thrilling than traditional action

### Technique

#### Slow Pace

Filled with subtext

#### Psychological Battle

Words carry significant weight

#### Simmering

Tension allowed to build slowly

### Character Depth

Dialogue reveals character even when not advancing plot

### Rhythm And Voice

Unique character voices, rhythmic speech

### Conflict Generation

Characters expressing differing opinions


## Mexican Standoff

### Definition

Multiple armed parties, no one can shoot first without risk

### Function

Generates anxiety and anticipation

### Visual Technique

Dramatic camera angles amplify tension

### Nature

Lose-lose situation, inherent tension

### Films

- Reservoir Dogs
- Pulp Fiction
- Inglourious Basterds

### Self Awareness

Inglourious Basterds defines and satirizes the concept
