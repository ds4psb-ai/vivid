# Quentin Tarantino Music Selection and Needle Drop Philosophy
**Auteur:** Quentin Tarantino | **Category:** sound_and_score | **ID:** tarantino_music_needle_drop

---

## Needle Drop Philosophy

### Definition

Integrating pre-existing songs into film soundtrack

### Process

#### Source

Personal extensive record collection

#### Timing

Often during writing phase

#### Selection

Tracks that embody personality and spirit of film

### Song Preference

#### Type

Deep cuts, obscure tracks from past eras

#### Belief

Established songs carry more emotional weight than new score

#### Quality

Timeless coolness

### Function

Not background - powerful storytelling tool

### Techniques

- Set mood
- Enhance narrative
- Create memorable scenes
- Ironic juxtaposition
