## Ensemble Approach

### Philosophy

Build worlds around trusted actor voices and styles

### Effect

Memorable, defining roles for performers


## Samuel L Jackson

### Status

Most prolific collaborator, cornerstone of film universe

### Pulp Fiction Impact

#### Role

Jules Winnfield - fast-talking, bible-quoting hitman

#### Awards

- BAFTA Best Supporting Actor
- Oscar nomination

#### Significance

Career turning point

### Films

#### Pulp Fiction

Jules Winnfield (1994)

#### Jackie Brown

Ordell Robbie - gun runner (1997)

#### Kill Bill Vol 2

Rufus - wedding musician cameo (2004)

#### Inglourious Basterds

Narrator - voice only (2009)

#### Django Unchained

Stephen - repugnant house slave villain (2012)

#### Hateful Eight

Major Marquis Warren - vengeful gunslinger (2015)

### Range

Anti-heroic to despicable roles


## Uma Thurman

### Status

Considered Tarantino's muse, especially for Kill Bill

### Mia Wallace

#### Role

Glamorous, mysterious mob boss wife

#### Film

Pulp Fiction (1994)

#### Awards

Oscar nomination

#### Significance

International prominence

### Beatrix Kiddo

#### Alias

- The Bride
- Black Mamba

#### Films

Kill Bill Vol 1 & 2 (2003-2004)

#### Origin

Conceived together during Pulp Fiction filming

#### Awards

- BAFTA nomination
- Golden Globe nomination

#### Preparation

Watched Coffy, Lady Snowblood

### Significance

One of most iconic director-actor duos in Hollywood
