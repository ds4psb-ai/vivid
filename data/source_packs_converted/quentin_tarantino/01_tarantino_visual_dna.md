# Quentin Tarantino Visual DNA - Signature Elements
**Auteur:** Quentin Tarantino | **Category:** visual_dna | **ID:** tarantino_visual_dna

---

## Cinematography Signature

### Trunk Shot

#### Technique

Low-angle from inside car trunk

#### Purpose

Power dynamic, POV of victim

#### Films

- Reservoir Dogs
- Pulp Fiction
- Kill Bill
- Jackie Brown

### Long Takes

#### Purpose

Build tension through unbroken scenes

#### Dialogue Scenes

Extended conversations, theatrical staging

### Lighting Approach

#### Style

Classic Hollywood backlighting

#### Hard Light

Strong shadows, dramatic contrast

#### Focus

Character-centric, not environmental realism

### Camera Angles

#### Low Angle

Power, menace

#### Dutch Angle

Unease, stylization

#### Crash Zoom

Dramatic emphasis, grindhouse homage


## Grindhouse Influence

### Exploitation Films

Raw aesthetic, B-movie energy

### Violence Stylization

Exaggerated, almost cartoonish

### Chapter Structure

Title cards, episodic narrative


## Dialogue Philosophy

### Extended Conversations

Tension through talk

### Pop Culture References

Music, movies, brands

### Profanity As Rhythm

Musicality of vulgar language

### Mundane To Violent

Sudden tonal shifts


## Thematic Obsessions

### Revenge

Elaborate payback narratives

### Honor Among Thieves

Criminal codes

### Feet

Recurring visual motif

### Nostalgia

1970s culture, vinyl, classic cars
