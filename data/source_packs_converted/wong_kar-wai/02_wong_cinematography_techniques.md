# Wong Kar-wai Cinematography Techniques - Christopher Doyle Collaboration
**Auteur:** Wong Kar-wai | **Category:** cinematography | **ID:** wong_cinematography_techniques

---

## Signature Techniques

### Step Printing

#### Definition

Printing individual frames multiple times in optical printer

#### Effect

Stuttering slow-motion, dreamlike quality

#### Films

- Chungking Express
- Happy Together
- In the Mood for Love

#### Example

Wide shot of protagonists standing in rain

### Undercranking

#### Definition

Shooting at lower frame rate than 24fps

#### Typical Rate

4-8 fps (Doyle: around 4-8fps for step-printing)

#### Effect

When played at 24fps: sped up, frenzied, motion blur

#### Combined With

Step-printing for stuttering effect with blur

### Time Manipulation

#### Purpose

Stretch, fragment, or intensify moments

#### Themes

Unfulfilled desire, lingering memories


## Framing Composition

### Frames Within Frames

#### Elements

Doorways, windows, mirrors

#### Purpose

Emotional confinement and isolation

#### Effect

Characters trapped within visual boundaries

### Off Center Positioning

#### Technique

Characters at edges of frame

#### Negative Space

Surrounding emptiness = emotional distance

#### Barriers

Visual representation of emotional walls

### Long Lenses

#### Effect

Compress space for intimacy despite emotional distance

#### Feeling

Close yet apart

### Voyeuristic Framing

#### Method

Grilled windows, partially obstructed views

#### Tracking Shots

Eye-level shots of characters' backsides

#### Audience Feeling

Spying on intimate moments

### Proscenium Framing

#### Style

Static shots, slow pans, subtle tracking

#### Handheld

Sparingly used for emotional tension peaks


## Lighting And Color

### Low Key Lighting

#### Method

Single light source, deep shadows

#### Mood

Melancholy, intimacy

### Tungsten Dominance

#### Warmth

Golden interior tones

#### Palette

Heavy magenta and cyan influx

### Monochromatic Stretching

#### Technique

One color stretched across luminance in single frame

#### Signature Colors

Deep reds, burnt oranges, jade greens
