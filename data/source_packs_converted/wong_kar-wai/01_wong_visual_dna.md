# Wong Kar-wai Visual DNA - Signature Elements
**Auteur:** Wong Kar-wai | **Category:** visual_dna | **ID:** wong_visual_dna

---

## Cinematography Signature

### Smudge Motion

#### Technique

Step-printing and undercranking

#### Effect

Colors seamlessly blend and blur

#### Emotional Purpose

Creates dreamlike, memory-like quality

#### Key Films

- Chungking Express
- Fallen Angels
- In the Mood for Love

### Color As Montage

#### Philosophy

Color carries narrative and emotional weight

#### Primary Palette

##### Deep Reds

Inner stirrings, fervent emotions, passion

##### Cool Blues

Melancholy, distance, urban isolation

##### Greens

Sadness, crumbling relationships

##### Neon Saturated

Urban energy, fleeting connections

#### Contrast Strategy

Juxtapose warm passion against cool loneliness

### Framing Techniques

#### Reflections

Mirrors, windows to fragment identity

#### Obstructed Views

Frames within frames, voyeuristic perspective

#### Tight Spaces

Cramped corridors, small apartments for intimacy


## Thematic Obsessions

### Time And Memory

Non-linear storytelling, dates as chapter markers

### Urban Loneliness

Hong Kong as character, neon-lit isolation

### Missed Connections

Ships passing in the night, unrequited love

### Nostalgia

1960s Hong Kong, period costumes, retro music


## Collaborators

### Christopher Doyle

#### Role

Primary cinematographer (1994-2000)

#### Contribution

Handheld energy, saturated colors, step-printing

### William Chang

#### Role

Art director, costume designer, editor

#### Contribution

Visual cohesion, period accuracy, rhythmic editing
