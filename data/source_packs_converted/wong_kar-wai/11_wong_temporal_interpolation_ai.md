## Expert Analysis

### Temporal Distortion Physics

#### Step Printing Mechanism

Duplicating frames (e.g., 1-1-2-2-3-3) to stretch time while maintaining kinetic energy

#### Undercranking Physics

Shooting 6-12 fps with 360-degree shutter

#### Visual Result

Smearing motion blur + jagged strobing cadence

#### Emotional Function

Subjective time dilation, memory fragmentation, urban isolation

### Frame Rate Psychology

#### Smoothness Paradox

Rejection of 24fps/60fps realism for subjective expressionism

#### Blur As Emotion

Motion blur magnitude correlates with character internal chaos


## Ai Video Generation Implications

### Interpolation Counter Strategy

#### Challenge

Standard AI (RIFE, FILM) aims for smoothing, destroying Wong's aesthetic

#### Solution

Reverse-engineering step-printing via 'frame-dropping' post-generation

#### Prompting Stuter

Prompting 'low frame rate', 'stroboscopic motion', 'long exposure shutter'

### Style Transfer Tokens

#### Temporal Tokens

step-printing, smear motion, ghosting trails, 12fps playback

#### Color Latency

Latent space color bleeding between frames to simulate film chemical transitions

### Generative Workflow

#### Technique

Generate at normal speed -> Algorithmically remove frames -> Apply optical flow smear -> Re-time to 24fps

#### Glitch Aesthetic

Intentional utilization of interpolation artifacts to mimic 'memory decay'
