# Bong Joon-ho Color Symbolism and Grading
**Auteur:** Bong Joon-ho | **Category:** color_theory | **ID:** bong_color_symbolism

---

## Color Philosophy

### Core Principle

Color as class division marker and psychological signal

### Approach

Contrasting palettes between social classes

### Desaturation

Often muted to appear less obvious, then reveals meaning


## Parasite Colors

### Warm Golden

#### Location

Park family mansion

#### Meaning

Comfort, affluence, carefree lifestyle

#### Lighting

Abundant natural light

### Cool Muted

#### Location

Kim family semi-basement

#### Meaning

Poverty, struggle, confinement

#### Lighting

Fluorescent greenish, dim tungsten

### Green

#### Symbolism

Wealth, deception, transformation

#### Contrast

Park mansion = natural subdued green, Kim home = artificial sickly green

#### Object

Peach as green fruit = tension between characters

### Red

#### Symbolism

Danger, violence, passion

#### Trigger

Appears when Kim plan goes awry

#### Final Scene

Ki-taek consumed by red and shadows = irreversible action

### Blue

#### Symbolism

Sadness, depression, deception, illusion

#### Usage

When poor family is introduced to wealthy family

#### Character

Housekeeper's failure and despair

### Orange

#### Bong Quote

Wanted scarlet-orange color as accent

#### Meaning

Unnerving vibration, imbalance, neurosis

#### Theme

Distress from dysfunctional capitalism

#### Location

Character Min, near basement door


## Memories Of Murder Colors

### Overall Palette

Muted and desaturated, dark and gloomy

### Green

#### Meaning

Mystery, malady, suspicion, blame

#### Locations

Green field (victims), interrogation room green door

### Red

#### Dual Meaning

Identifies potential victims AND marks red herrings

#### Purpose

Generate confusion and mystery

### Blue

#### Meaning

Neutral observers, bystanding townspeople

#### Visual

Blue sky overlooking events

### Restoration

Criterion added green tint (Bong-approved)


## Mother Colors

### Palette

Muted browns, greens, blues = dreary atmosphere

### Purple

#### Usage

Mother's 'weird purple clothes' in opening

#### Meaning

Madness, fate, tragedy

### Wide Shots

Mother small against landscape = isolation

### Bw Version

Release suggests noir and Hitchcockian elements
