# Bong Joon-ho Cinematography Techniques - Hong Kyung-pyo Collaboration
**Auteur:** Bong Joon-ho | **Category:** cinematography | **ID:** bong_cinematography_techniques

---

## Camera Equipment

### Camera

ARRI ALEXA 65

### Lenses

ARRI Rental Prime DNA lenses + Hasselblad

### Effect

Almost anamorphic feel with deep depth of field

### Field Of View

25mm equivalent on 35mm format, optical character of 50mm


## Camera Movement Symbolism

### Infiltration Motion

#### Technique

Subtle forward camera motion (imperceptible)

#### Meaning

Kim family's calculated infiltration and growing influence

#### Creates

Sense of foreboding

### Chaos Moments

#### Technique

Erratic handheld camera work

#### Example

Return of the ousted housekeeper

### Class Distinction

#### Park Family

Steadicam + fluid movements = freedom and vastness

#### Kim Family

Locked-off shots = confinement and limitation

### Long Takes

#### Description

Seamlessly combine wide, medium, close-up without cuts

#### Purpose

Naturalistic perspective, objective viewpoint


## Composition Techniques

### Spatial Division

#### Method

Physical boundaries (windows, walls, height variations)

#### Purpose

Visual separation of families within frame

### Stairs Metaphor

#### Upward

Social climbing, aspiration

#### Downward

Descent into nothingness, failure

#### Source Inspiration

Akira Kurosawa's 'High and Low'

### Window Symbolism

#### Kim Basement

Small window with restricted view

#### Park Mansion

Large expansive window (2.39:1 ratio), overlooks garden

#### Bong Description

Window mimics a CinemaScope screen

### Framing By Class

#### Poor

Close-ups, tight framing = limitations

#### Rich

Medium/wide shots, fluid camera = affluence

### Decalcomania

#### Meaning

Technique creating symmetrical images

#### Application

Geometric patterns, lines, shapes for narrative

### Proscenium Framing

#### Style

Front-on framing like stage play

#### Effect

Impartial viewpoint for audience
