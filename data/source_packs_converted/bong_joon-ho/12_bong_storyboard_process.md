# Bong Joon-ho Storyboard and Pre-visualization Process
**Auteur:** Bong Joon-ho | **Category:** filmmaking_process | **ID:** bong_storyboard_process

---

## Storyboard Philosophy

### Meticulous Approach

Hand-draws comprehensive storyboards for every single shot

### Detail Level

#### Includes

- Background settings
- Kinetic compositions
- Mise-en-scène
- Camera angles
- Actor movements

#### Resembles

Manga-style visual novels

### Personality Driven

#### Bong Quote

Has to draw storyboards due to anxious personality

#### Function

Reduces anxiety, provides clear roadmap


## Pre Visualization

### Mental Pre Edit

#### Approach

Draws entire movie in brain from offset

#### Technique

Pre-edits film through storyboards

### On Set Efficiency

#### Minimal Coverage

Only captures necessary, planned takes

#### Minimal Improv

Follows storyboards closely during filming

### Crew Communication

#### Distribution

Actors and crew receive storyboard packets

#### Visual Blueprint

Complete visual plan of entire film


## Parasite Storyboards

### Compilation

Published as graphic novel

### Detail

So complete they function as standalone narrative

### Production Use

Crew used as reference throughout production


## Preparation Timeline

### Concept Development

Years before script (4 years for Parasite concept)

### Script Writing

Relatively fast (4 months for Parasite)

### Set Mockups

Creates mock-ups during writing for production design


## Actor Perspective

### Song Kang Ho View

Allows full concentration on performance

### Chris Evans View

Frees actors from technical worries

### Trust

Actors trust storyboard precision
