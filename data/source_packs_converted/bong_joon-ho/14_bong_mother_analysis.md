## Cinematography Techniques

### Telephoto Profile Shot

#### Usage

Significant story moments

#### Effect

Positions viewer further from characters

#### Emotion Access

Makes emotions less accessible

#### Audience Role

Turns viewer into witness who cannot intervene

### Minimizing Cuts

#### Technique

Coordinated blocking + camera pans instead of cuts

#### Dialogue Scenes

Avoids traditional shot-reverse-shot

#### Effect

Greater environmental depth and spatial awareness

### Telephoto Compression

#### Urban Settings

Cramped spaces despite wide aspect ratio

#### Visual Effect

Compressed visual space

### Shot Reverse Shot Usage

#### Frequency

Sparingly used, especially in later scenes

#### Effect

When used, carries more gravity and tension


## Maternal Sacrifice Theme

### Protagonist

Kim Hye-ja as Mother

### Son

Intellectually disabled Yoon Do-joon

### Core Conflict

Proving son's innocence in murder case

### Bong Perspective

#### Excessive Love

Can become madness

#### Corruption

Motherly love becomes morally compromising

### Subversion

Challenges traditional image of motherhood


## Psychological Thriller Elements

### Ambiguity

Visual techniques reinforce unreliable truth

### Mystery

Audience attempts to understand with limited info

### Moral Complexity

Mother's actions increasingly compromising

### Dark Humor

Interwoven with tension throughout
