## Heptapod Language

### Design

#### Type

Circular, non-linear

#### Contrast

Human linear languages

#### Creator

Martine Bertrand (Villeneuve's wife) - aesthetics

### Linguistic Consultant

#### Name

Jessica Coon

#### Role

Advised on field linguistics approach

#### Actor Prep

Met with Amy Adams to discuss linguistics work

### Sapir Whorf Hypothesis

#### Concept

Language shapes perception and thought

#### Application

Learning Heptapod B rewires brain for non-linear time


## Circular Time

### Heptapod Perception

Past, present, future experienced simultaneously

### No Beginning Or End

All of time at once

### Louise Transformation

#### Process

Immersion in Heptapod B

#### Result

Experiences memories of future (flash-forwards)

### Editing Technique

#### Deliberate Non Linear

What appear as flashbacks are future glimpses

#### Purpose

Immerse audience in altered time perception


## Heptapod Design

### Goal

Truly alien, distinct from typical cinematic aliens

### Tone

Sympathetic yet subtly unsettling

### Inspirations

#### Creatures

- Cuttlefish
- Squids
- Microscopic organisms

#### Eyes

Thoughtful qualities of horse eyes

#### Daughter

Villeneuve's daughter's octopus drawing

### Final Design

#### Form

Massive seven-legged creatures (Heptapods)

#### Communication

Six-limbed 'hand' + inky black circular symbols

#### Presentation

Mostly through hand behind mist


## Spaceship Design

### Initial Concept

Oyster-shaped

### Final Design

#### Shape

Oval inspired by exoplanet Haumea

#### Orientation

Vertical, hovering

#### Purpose

Emphasize human journey upward to meet them
