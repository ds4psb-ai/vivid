# Denis Villeneuve Blade Runner 2049 - World Building and Cinematography
**Auteur:** Denis Villeneuve | **Category:** film_analysis | **ID:** villeneuve_blade_runner_2049

---

## World Building Philosophy

### Approach

Period movie - natural progression from 1982 original

### Environment As Character

True

### Dystopia Vision

#### Quality

Brutality - harsh environment vs imposing architecture

#### Inspiration

Smog-laden cities like Beijing

#### Architecture

Stark minimal Brutalist style

### Evolution From Original

More desolate, bleaker, somber atmosphere

### Neon Streets

Still iconic but with bleaker tone


## Practical Sets

### Percentage

90% constructed practically

### Rationale

#### Actors

Perform within tangible environment

#### Vfx

Integrate seamlessly, appear real

### Pre Production

#### Storyboarding

Weeks with Deakins and 2 artists in Montreal

#### Tool Preference

Pencils for concept art


## Winter Elements

### Choice

Pervasive snow in LA 2049

### Villeneuve Influence

Canadian background

### Thematic Function

Reinforces brutality theme


## Temporal Distancing

### Technique

Defunct company advertisements (Pan Am, Atari)

### Effect

Sense of distance from present, alternate history


## Roger Deakins Cinematography

### Collaboration

Third with Villeneuve

### Description

Most technically demanding project

### Control

Full artistic control, rejected multi-camera setups

### Aesthetic

Noir-ish with masterful light and color


## Color Palettes

### Contrast

#### City

Stark black and blue

#### Desert

Warm orange hues

### Function

Delineate environments and themes

### In Camera

Achieved color effects primarily in-camera


## Camera Specs

### Camera

Arri Alexa Plus

### Focal Lengths

Mid-range preferred, wide as 14mm for space

### Effects

Extensive fog and atmospheric


## Lighting Integration

### Collaboration

Deakins + production designer Dennis Gassner

### Method

Lighting integrated directly into set design
